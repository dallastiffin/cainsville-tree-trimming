About Cainsville Tree Service

What Makes Us Different

Nothing begins without paperwork. Each tree trimming quote identifies the trees one by one, explains what happens to each, gives the share of crown coming off, and confirms the destination of brush and timber.

Where no tree trimming is warranted, we say so. There is no charge for hearing that, and we prefer it to taking on a job we cannot justify.

Our Commitment To Quality

Cut placement matters, and ours are positioned so the tree can close the wound. Topping is a different thing altogether, it is not on offer here, and we will happily explain why if somebody else has proposed it.

Insurance covers our tree trimming and tree removal work, and the paperwork is available before anyone starts. Ask every firm bidding on your trees to produce theirs.

Working With Local Conditions

The soil around Cainsville is a shallow layer of sand over clay. Roots cannot get down through the clay, so they spread sideways into a wide flat plate.

This changes what tree trimming needs to achieve. Reducing crown weight matters more here than it would on deeper ground, and the timing matters too, because a saturated root plate holds far less firmly than a dry one.

Deciding whether it is cut back or taken out is free. Finding out costs you nothing at all.

How A Job Runs

Four steps apply to every tree trimming job we take, without exception:

- Somebody attends and inspects the tree in person before a figure is written - A written tree trimming quote follows, naming each tree, each cut, and the fate of the debris - The site is prepared first, with anything breakable moved well out of the way - Brush goes through the chipper, timber is stacked or loaded as agreed, and the ground is raked over

Our range extends to Echo Place, Middleport, Newport, Onondaga and eastern Brantford. If you are close to the boundary of that, put the question to us anyway.
