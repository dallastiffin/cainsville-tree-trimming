What Are The Benefits Of Tree Trimming?

✓

Two things make tree trimming worthwhile. It takes weight and wind resistance out of the crown, which eases the strain on the roots, and it brings dead wood down before the tree drops it without warning.

✓

A third benefit matters most on young trees. Correcting a weak fork while the branch is still thin takes minutes, and it prevents a problem that would later need steel or a stump.

Tree trimming information for Cainsville property owners

Why Do Trees Here Fall Over Instead Of Breaking?

The soil in Cainsville is a shallow layer of sand sitting on clay. Roots cannot push through the clay, so they spread sideways into a wide flat plate rather than anchoring downward.

A tree held by a flat plate does not usually snap in a storm. It tips over complete, with the root plate lifting out of the ground. Regular tree trimming reduces the leverage that causes this, which is why tree trimming matters more here than in most places.

When Is The Risk Of A Tree Falling Highest?

Cainsville sits inside a flood reach that the County maps from Paris down through Onondaga, and the ground at The Oxbow is river alluvium left by the water.

Wet soil holds a root plate far more loosely than dry soil does, which affects when tree trimming should be booked. The risk is therefore seasonal rather than constant, and it peaks in spring and after a long wet spell, which is also when strong winds tend to arrive.

Why Do Permit Rules Change Halfway Through Cainsville?

Cainsville is split down the middle. Roughly half falls under the City of Brantford and half under the County of Brant, and the two have different tree by-laws.

The boundary does not follow anything you can see from the street, so your neighbour's answer may not apply to your property. Give us your address before booking and we will tell you which authority to contact.

How Does Nearby Construction Affect Trees?

Water and wastewater construction is scheduled east of Cainsville between 2027 and 2029. A trench dug beside a tree cuts the roots along one side of it.

A tree already standing on a shallow plate has very little to spare. If work is planned near a tree you want to keep, book the tree trimming beforehand, while there is still room to work and the tree still has its full anchorage.

What Does Tree Trimming Cost In Cainsville?

What drives a tree trimming figure is the scale of the tree, whatever sits in the fall zone, and how far the truck ends up from the trunk. Almost every caller starts with how tall it is, which is the least informative detail of the three.

Open lawn around a tree keeps the job simple. Squeeze the same tree between two houses with a service line running through the branches and the hours multiply. We put the figure in writing once someone has seen it.

Our Process

What Happens During A Tree Trimming Visit?

  1. The visit starts with the whole property rather than the single tree that prompted your call. Lots in Cainsville were generally planted in one go, so whatever is wrong with one tree is often developing in its neighbours.

  2. After that we assess the ground beneath, since that governs the tree trimming approach. Grass underneath means a limb can simply be let go. Shingles, fencing or a vehicle mean it descends under control.

  3. Afterwards the brush is chipped, the timber is stacked or loaded according to what you chose, and the ground is raked over. You should not be able to tell we were there apart from the tree.
