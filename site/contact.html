What Should I Tell You When I Call?

Five details will get you an accurate tree trimming figure and usually remove the need for us to return:

- The tree in question, with its height compared against something familiar such as your roofline - Your address, because Cainsville is split between two municipalities with different by-laws - Whatever occupies the ground below, whether that is shingles, paving, fencing or grass - The symptom as you would describe it, perhaps a bare limb or a trunk that has started to tilt - The width of the entrance, and whether there is room for a truck to turn

What Happens After I Get In Touch?

A time is arranged for someone to come and inspect the tree before any tree trimming is priced. The visit is free and commits you to nothing.

What follows is a written tree trimming quote covering the trees individually, the work intended for each, the destination of brush and timber, and every exclusion.

What If A Tree Is On A Hydro Line?

Call the utility first, not us. Until they have made the line safe the tree remains their responsibility, and no tree trimming crew should touch it before that point.

Before they arrive, treat every object in contact with the tree as dangerous, fencing and vehicles included.

Where Do We Work?

We serve Cainsville, Echo Place, Middleport, Newport, Onondaga and the east end of Brantford.

If you are sitting slightly beyond those limits, ask anyway, because the scale of the work counts for more than the mileage. The home page lists everything we offer.
